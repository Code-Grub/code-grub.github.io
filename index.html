Q & A

Where is the venue?

We are getting married at the beautiful historic Newcastle Community Hall at 20 King Street West, Newcastle, Ontario, Canada. The ceremony will take place at the gazebo located next to the main hall.

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes — there is limited onsite parking. Due to the limited nature of the parking, we are hoping to ensure our senior family members are able to park there. If you have any questions, do not hesitate to contact the bride or groom! There is additional parking on the street and local public parking nearby.

Is there a registry?

No, we do not have a registry. We kindly ask for cash gifts only to help us in the future.
